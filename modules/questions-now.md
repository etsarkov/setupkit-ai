### NOW-01 — NOW

Question: What has already been done on the chosen task, and what needs your attention next?

Why: For example, materials are collected but you have not started writing, or work is waiting for someone else's reply. A short update is enough.

Effect / if deferred: The assistant can continue from where you are instead of starting over. If the current situation is unclear, propose a small check or first step. Mark the current status as unknown; do not claim earlier work is complete.

Activate / reuse: The chosen current-context/task scenario needs present status or focus and no sufficiently current confirmed record supplies it. Reuse confirmed applicable answers and selected sources first. Relevant IDs: CORE-02, GOAL-01, GOAL-06, ORG-14. A stated goal does not establish progress. Reuse a supplied project stage/status and ask only the missing focus or changed fact.
