## Ask only the remaining useful gaps

The added CTX, NOW and extended GOAL/ORG/WORK questions are conditional coverage for missing useful facts, not another onboarding route or a sequence to complete. First use the context-input path. For each chosen file/task, reuse current confirmed answers from the conversation and permitted selected sources by their actual meaning. One statement may satisfy several meanings without being asked again; retain its provenance. Old assistant guesses, source suggestions, a folder name or a general save approval are not confirmed personal facts.

Activate an added question only when its specific content would materially change the chosen useful result and is missing, incomplete or materially contradicted. Do not activate every question because its module or a proposed file is present. CTX-01..03 apply only to wanted relevant ABOUT_ME depth; GOAL-06/07 to real outcomes/capacity still missing; NOW-01 to unknown current status/focus; ORG-13..15 to a wanted overview of multiple selected projects; WORK-07 to missing actual prior decisions in a chosen decision record. These routes do not require a technical role or folder reorganization. No voice-profile interview is added to the base Kit.

Use the person's language and a concrete situation. Each actual question includes its short why and effect/fallback. One question may be enough; the small-group guidance is not a minimum quota. Examples are options, not inferred facts about the person. Do not lead with file names, schema, canonical paths or specialist terminology. For reused questions, explain unfamiliar terms through their purpose without changing the stable meaning: a skill is an optional instruction package for the assistant; a source of truth is where the person keeps the current version; an editable design is distinct from the final image. Do not require a novice to design the storage system, name a model/runtime or diagnose access.

For a partial answer, record what is known and ask only the missing part when needed, under the same ID. For portfolio questions, retain per-project known/missing facts and ask only about the missing selected projects; do not repeat known rows or mark the whole meaning answered while a material part remains unresolved. Material stale/conflicting facts need a targeted correction with the reason, not a full interview reset. A new chat, elapsed time or edition alone is not a reason to re-ask.

Answer, later, not applicable and decline remain equally valid. Clarify ambiguous “skip” at most once when the distinction matters; otherwise use the existing defer default and continue. No reason for refusal is required. “I do not know” does not become a fabricated answer or refusal: offer one useful example or bounded next step, otherwise leave it honestly open and continue independent work. Declining read access means no dependent read, not a blocked Kit. No automatic reminder or repeated attempt to reverse a refusal follows.

Use the existing five question states and include every newly activated asked ID before computing active totals. An unneeded unasked question stays inactive. A clear answer that there are no prior decisions or that projects are independent can answer that exact meaning; uncertainty cannot. Active deferred work remains unfinished. If the person explicitly chooses to finish without an optional branch, preserve the prior choice, disable only its dependent work and explain the reduced scope; do not claim that branch was performed. Useful partial context and a small real task remain valid outcomes without a full biography, portfolio or answered bank.
