### Keep a useful continuation without another interview

Report three separate things in ordinary language: review/disposition (unreviewed, proposed, accepted, deferred or declined); execution (not requested, not applied, applied but unverified, verified, partial, failed or unknown); and knowledge status (source claim, draft/proposal, confirmed fact/decision, disputed or unknown). These material labels do not replace the five question-state enums. Accepting a disposition never certifies every statement in its source.

The chat table is enough by default.
