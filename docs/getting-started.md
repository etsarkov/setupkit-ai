# How to start

Copy the start prompt, paste it into your current AI chat and send it. Answer your assistant's questions and choose a useful task. No SetupKit signup or app installation is needed just to supply text and review a draft. Your assistant's actual tools, costs and data rules still apply.

## If the guide cannot be read

Open [the complete current text](https://setupkit.ai/kit/) and copy the complete text, including its final marker. Paste it into the same chat. If it is too long, paste numbered parts in order. Wait until all parts are supplied, then send the completion instruction shown on that page. The assistant should confirm the edition, date, complete sections and final marker, reuse your answers and continue from your last confirmed step. It must ask for missing parts instead of reconstructing them.

If the website is unavailable, open [the complete guide in this repository](../STARTER.md) and copy its text instead. Do not connect an account or bypass restrictions just to retrieve the guide.

## When another work mode is useful

If your task needs tools or file access, ask what the current chat can do first. You can continue manually, defer the step or choose another work mode. If you move, ask for a reviewed continuation summary with the relevant answers, sources and open questions. Keep it with your own work. Do not send it to SetupKit. Reading instructions does not grant permission to install, save, send or change anything.

## Return without starting over

Bring your latest saved progress or continuation, including the last Kit edition you completed if known. Your assistant should reuse confirmed answers and review only relevant unfinished or new steps. If that history is unavailable, compare what is known without assuming you must start over. You can skip or defer a question; only work that depends on its answer needs to wait.

If you want reusable context, start with selected material you already have and review the summary before saving. Organization, personal files and progress saving are separate choices. With no file access, a useful result and a copyable continuation in the conversation are still possible. A summary alone is not the complete guide or a complete transfer package. Follow the guide’s transfer steps when changing assistants; keep private material and permission grants out of inappropriate destinations.

## A brief welcome, then your next step

After loading the complete guide, your assistant gives a short introduction in your language: what you can achieve together, how to answer in ordinary words, and how to skip or pause. If you already supplied your goal, it uses that goal.

When you return with relevant continuation available, the assistant briefly picks up the known next step. If that context is unavailable, it asks only for what is needed rather than claiming to remember. A new Kit edition does not require repeating the introduction or your previous answers.
